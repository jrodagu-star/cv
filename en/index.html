Professional profile

Specialist in Intensive Care Medicine, trained at Hospital General Universitario Gregorio Marañón, with 15 years of ICU experience focused on cardiovascular, neurological and postoperative critical care. Member of the advanced circulatory support team within the cardiac surgery programme at Hospital HM Montepríncipe. Combines clinical practice with the development of digital tools for clinical management and critical care education, with a particular interest in ultrasound and echocardiography in critically ill patients.
