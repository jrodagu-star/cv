Profile

Professional summary

Consultant in Intensive Care Medicine, trained at the Gregorio Marañón University Hospital, with 15 years of experience in intensive care units in Madrid and 7 previous years in emergency medicine and primary care. I currently work full time at HM Puerta del Sur Hospital (specialised in neurological pathology and post-surgical care) and, over recent years, at Madrid Montepríncipe Hospital (cardiac patients, post-surgical and cardiac surgery). My current interests focus on ultrasound and echocardiography in the critically ill patient and on the development of databases for management, evaluation and clinical research, and the use of AI for clinical purposes.
